Financial Services Offered

Money Transfer

We facilitate both **local transfers (OMT Intra)** and **international transfers (Western Union)**. Send and receive funds quickly and securely through our extensive national network of agents.

Bill and Government Payments

OMT simplifies daily life by allowing users to pay utility bills, telecommunication bills, and various **government fees and fines** directly at any OMT location. This includes payments for traffic tickets and essential municipal services.

Digital Solutions

Our digital offerings, including the **OMT Pay mobile wallet** and the OMT Visa Card, provide modern solutions for online shopping, in-store payments, and contactless transactions, keeping your finances secure and accessible.
