Our Story

Founded in 1985, Vintage Canvas began as a passion project to preserve and showcase the often-overlooked masterpieces of the mid-20th century. Our founder, Elena Marchetti, discovered a collection of forgotten photographs in her grandmother's attic that sparked a lifelong dedication to preserving this unique artistic period.

Today, our gallery houses over 500 pieces from renowned artists of the 1940s through 1960s, focusing on works created in monochrome and limited color palettes. We believe these pieces capture a particular emotional depth and timelessness that resonates with contemporary audiences.

Each artwork in our collection has been carefully restored and preserved using archival techniques to ensure these treasures remain accessible for future generations. Our team includes specialists in conservation, art history, and curatorial studies.
