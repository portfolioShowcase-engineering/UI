Our History and Story

Beginnings in Money Transfer

OMT started its journey in 1998 in partnership with Western Union, establishing itself as the **primary agent in Lebanon** for international money transfers. This partnership quickly built a vast network of service points, giving access to quick and reliable financial services across the country.

Growth and Diversification

Following its success in money transfer, OMT expanded its vision to become a comprehensive financial service provider. We focused on localizing services to meet the daily needs of the Lebanese community, introducing **bill payment, government fee collection, and other essential services**.

A National Network

Today, OMT operates the **largest and most widespread network** of local agents, ensuring that financial access is available everywhere from major cities to the most remote towns. Our commitment is to simplicity, security, and convenience for all our users.
